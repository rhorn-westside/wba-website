## The shape of the year

The school year runs in four nine-week grading periods, two per semester. Progress reports go home four and a half weeks into each quarter; report cards at the end of each.

## Weather and emergency closures

When school closes for bad weather or another emergency, check in this order:

1. Email and text through **Schoolcast**
2. The school **Facebook page** and this website
3. Your child's **teacher**
4. **Mr. Horn**, (832) 294-1921 — only after the first three
