## Chapel

Chapel is a regular part of the school week — singing, Scripture and preaching, with the whole school together. Students in 6th grade and above wear a tie and dress shirt on chapel days, and every student brings a Bible.

## Fine arts

Fine arts is treated as a vital part of preparing a well-rounded student — developing talents and preparing students for a broader future serving the Lord. It is not an after-school extra.

- Choir
- Orchestra
- Junior High Ensemble
- Senior High Ensemble
- Art
- Speech
- Private instrumental lessons

**All students are required to participate in the In-School Fine Arts Competition.** Students in K5 through 5th grade memorize an approved poem or reading for competition. Students in 6th through 12th grade participate in and attend the regional competition — the MSAACS competition, held March 4–5 in Weatherford, Texas.

Electives such as choir, art and ensemble sometimes carry additional fees.

## Programs

Through the year students take part in dramatic, choral and instrumental programs. Attendance and faithful participation are required until a program is completed, and **participation is a major part of the music grade**.

Please let your pastor and church leaders know about these commitments, and mark the dates clearly so nothing conflicts.

[Program dates &rarr;](/families/calendar/)

## Athletics

The Warriors field flag football, with registration and physical evaluation forms published each season. Participation in school trips and athletics requires a good behavior record, appropriate academic standing, and a current financial account.

A student who is absent or suspended for the day may not participate in any school activity that day.

[Athletics forms &rarr;](/families/forms/)

## Physical education

Students are expected to participate unless excused by a doctor's note, and to dress out in the complete uniform. The cost of one required P.E. shirt is included in the gym fee; additional shirts are purchased at the start of the year, and culottes for girls are purchased through the school.

## Lunch

Students bring their own lunches. If a student arrives without one, the school provides a lunch and charges it to the family account. Microwaves and refrigerators are not available to students, and deliveries are discouraged except on rare special occasions.

Students may purchase soda, juice, candy, cookies and chips at the snack shop. Students do not share lunches or ask other students for food.

**Parents are welcome to eat lunch with their child** and may bring outside food when they do.

Plain lunch boxes are best — lunch boxes picturing rock stars, movie stars or undesirable television characters are not permitted.

## Birthdays

Cakes or cupcakes may be brought for the whole class to share at lunchtime; check with the teacher in advance. Birthday party invitations are not distributed at school unless the entire class (elementary) or grade (junior and senior high) is invited.

## Dress code

Students wear uniforms, ordered from the French Toast website using the stock numbers posted by the school. Shirts are white, grey or burgundy.

[Full dress code &rarr;](/student-life/dress-code/)

## Conduct

Attendance at the Academy is a privilege and not a right, and students are asked to hold a standard of conduct both on and off campus.

[Standard of conduct &rarr;](/student-life/conduct/)

## School trips

Deportment on trips should bring honor to the Lord and to the school. Students remain with the group, treat vehicles with respect, stay seated while the vehicle is moving, and sit in separate seats by sex. Students travel to and from an activity in the same vehicle unless a parent is present or another approved adult drives them, and the person in charge is notified either way.

Elementary and pre-school children may not ride to ball games with older brothers and sisters travelling with the school group.

## Summer reading

The Academy runs a summer reading program with assigned and recommended titles by grade level.
