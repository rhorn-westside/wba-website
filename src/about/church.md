## Building and equipping Christ-like families

That is the stated mission of Westside Baptist Church, and the Academy is one of the ways the church carries it out. The school shares the church's campus, its pastor, its doctrinal position and its purpose. A family enrolling a student here is joining something larger than a school.

Families are welcome at any service, whether or not they are members.
