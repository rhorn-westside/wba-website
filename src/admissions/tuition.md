## How payment works

Ten monthly payments, due on the **15th of each month**, beginning **August 15** with the final payment due **May 15**.

Payments may be sent by **Zelle to wbabillpay@mywestside.org**, or brought or mailed to the school office.

Requests for special payment arrangements must be submitted in writing. Any arrangement agreed to is signed by both the administration and the parents.

**Books and supplies are not included in tuition.** They are purchased separately from BJU Press or The Homeschool Store, using the book and material list for your child's grade.

## Late payments

| | |
|---|---|
| Not paid by the 25th of the month | $25.00 late charge |
| Account unpaid after 30 days | 1% penalty added |
| Account unpaid after 60 days | A further 1% added |
| Account 30 calendar days delinquent | Student may be suspended until the account is paid in full or satisfactory arrangements are made |

All accounts must be current as of May 15. It is school policy to ask parents to withdraw their students if the account is not current on that date.

While an account is delinquent the school may hold report cards, transcripts and standardized testing scores — including transcript requests — until it is paid in full.

## Other charges

| | |
|---|---|
| Returned check | $25.00 |
| Damaged books, equipment or property | Minimum $25.00, parent's responsibility |
| Additional transcripts | First is free, then $20.00 each |
| Lost and found items | $0.50 per item, claimable for two weeks |

**Private music lessons** may be available at a separate cost, paid directly to the instructor. Ask the office.

**Physical education uniform (K–12)** — the cost of one required uniform shirt is included in the gym fee. Additional shirts are purchased at the start of the year. Culottes for girls must be purchased through the school.

**Nominal expenses** — electives such as choir, art and ensemble sometimes carry additional fees. Class parties and field trips may have small costs. Athletic programs may require additional fees during a season.

## Withdrawal

Parents are responsible for tuition through the current semester of withdrawal or expulsion.

A student is officially withdrawn when a signed written statement is brought **in person** to the office — emails are not accepted. Notify the office in writing as soon as you know the date.

When records are requested at withdrawal, the account must be paid in full. Allow the office one full week to process the withdrawal form, and one month to issue transcripts to other institutions.

## Tax considerations

The school does not provide a contribution report for tuition or fees; these are payments and do not qualify as contributions. Because the Academy is a school and not a day care service, tuition payments do not qualify for the child and dependent care credit.

**Gifts** designated for the school ministry are a different matter — they are used solely for that purpose and are tax-deductible. [More about giving &rarr;](/give/)

## Why we publish this

Some schools quote tuition only on inquiry. We would rather a family know up front whether the Academy is within reach, instead of investing in a tour and an application to find out at the end. If cost is the only thing standing between your family and this school, call the office and say so.
