Westside Baptist Academy is a ministry, not a business. Tuition is set to keep a Christian education within reach of ordinary families, which means it covers the essentials and not much beyond them. Gifts to the Academy close that gap.

## Where gifts go

There is often a need for new equipment during the year. Gifts designated for the school ministry are used solely for that purpose, and are **tax-deductible**.

Note that tuition and fees are payments rather than contributions — the school does not issue a contribution report for them, and they do not qualify for the child and dependent care credit. Gifts are a separate matter.
